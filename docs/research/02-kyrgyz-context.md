# 02 - Local context in Kyrgyzstan

Status labels: **Verified** = source page read or its text retrieved; **Partly verified** = found in a search snippet or secondary source, or the match is only approximate; **Unverified** = nothing credible found.
All sources were accessed on 2026-10-29. Several figures are 2022-2023 and may be outdated.

## Summary

- **CVD share of deaths.** The spec's "50-52%" is close to official data: 52.4% (16.5k of 31.5k deaths) in 2023 per the National Statistical Committee (NSC); 52.1% in 2022 per a 2024 EHJ abstract; 52.3% quoted by the NCCIM director in March 2026. Verified/Partly verified.
- **Deaths per year.** "17-20k" is slightly high. Recent counts are 16.5-16.9k (2023-2024). The "20,000" figure in a MoH quote refers to premature CVD deaths at ages 30-60, which looks inconsistent with the totals. Treat "~17k" as the safe value.
- **"234.5 per 100,000" could not be traced.** It is not an age-standardised rate in any source we found. The World Heart Observatory gives a crude rate of 230 and an age-standardised rate of 429 (2024). The spec's number may be a crude rate with a wrong attribution.
- **Hypertension "34-37%" does not match.** WHO STEPS 2013 gives 42.9% for ages 25-64. No NCCIM source for 34-37% was found.
- **Rural share 65.1%** is verified (NSC yearbook 2023).
- **IHD rank "3rd women / 5th men of 51" is not verified.** A WHO review says Kyrgyzstan has the third-highest IHD mortality overall in the WHO European Region, with no sex split.
- **The "untapped lever" WHO/World Bank quote was not found.** Telemedicine is a stated government and UN priority, but the exact phrase should be dropped or reworded.
- **FAP count is understated.** The spec says "980+"; official figures are 1,057-1,059 (2020-2023).
- **Competing and complementary state initiatives exist.** In 2025-2026 the MoH and NCCIM announced AI-ECG "Digital FAPs" (SberMedAI, 200 planned) and tele-ECG to an expert centre. The World Bank is funding ECGs for PHC (additional financing approved March 2025, running to June 2027). Real policy and procurement alignment is therefore needed.
- **Connectivity is good on paper.** ITU reports 99.4% population 4G coverage in 2024, but offline mode is still justified for the last ~1% of settlements and for weak signal in the mountains.
- **Android dominates.** StatCounter gives Android 70-84% depending on month, with Xiaomi and Samsung leading. Target Android first.

## 2. Health-system structure

- **Primary care network (WHO review 2022, version read via snippets).** 1,059 FAPs run by feldshers or nurses, in nearly 90% of villages; the area served is about 500-2,000 people. There are 17 FMCs (7 oblast-level, 10 in Bishkek) and 61 GP centres with beds after the 2021 reorganisation. A family doctor serves 4,000-5,000 people in cities and more than 7,000 in remote areas ([WHO review](https://iris.who.int/bitstream/handle/10665/363175/9789289059237-eng.pdf); [Observatory](https://eurohealthobservatory.who.int/countries/kyrgyzstan)). Partly verified. The older 2018 WHO text listed 64 FMCs, 28 GP centres and 1,030 FAPs, which shows the network was reorganised ([WHO 2018](https://iris.who.int/server/api/core/bitstreams/5d6baa9e-ec99-486c-a1e1-be46052a4e80/content)).
- **Physicians.** WHO 2022: 16 per 10,000. NSC says about 19 per 10,000. 2021 range: 225-246 per 100,000 in Bishkek/Osh and about 70 in some rural areas. A UN article says staffing in rural areas is about half of urban areas ([Joint SDG Fund](https://www.jointsdgfund.org/article/bridging-gap-how-telemedicine-transforming-access-health-care-kyrgyzstan)). Partly verified.
- **Cardiologists.** No regional cardiologist headcount was found (Naryn, Talas, Batken, Osh/Alay: **not found**). One search tool reported about 48 per million nationally (ESC dataset, 2022; unverified). The spec's claim that the mountain regions are under-served is plausible but not quantified.
- **Cardiology service reform.** NCCIM runs "Acute MI 24/7" ("more than a thousand patients treated") and "Emergency Cardiology 2023-…". Regional cath-lab ("KATLAB") centres: equipment bought for Balykchy, Talas and Naryn (24.kg, 2025). The Naryn centre is planned with Aga Khan Foundation. NCCIM did 3,277 high-tech interventions in H1 2026 against 2,052 in H1 2025. The MoH describes "an integrated system for CVD diagnosis at district level using digital technologies" for the most remote villages ([24.kg](https://24.kg/en/article/341298); [K-News 2026-08](https://knews.kg/2026/08/22/minzdrav-v-kyrgyzstane-rasshiryaetsya-set-regionalnyh-katlab-tsentrov/); [Open.kg](https://open.kg/en/news/exclusive/37466-v-naryne-sozdadut-pervyj-v-strane-gornyj-vysokotehnologichnyj-gospital-gde-budut-lechit-serdechno-sosudistye-zabolevanija.html)). Partly verified (news items).
- **Emergency transport.** A 2018 WHO/MoH framework (Russian edition) states the ambulance service does not meet modern standards and is unevenly distributed, with limited access in remote areas ([WHO/UN framework 2018](https://kyrgyzstan.un.org/sites/default/files/2019-12/A%20service%20framework%20and%20roadmap%20for%20the%20development%20of%20care%20systems%20for%20heart%20attack%20and%20stroke%20in%20Kyrgyzstan%20%282018%29_RUS.pdf)). **May be outdated (2018)**; no current response-time data found.

## 5. Digital health and AI initiatives

- **Policy stack.** "Healthy Person - Prosperous Country 2019-2030" (amended Dec 2024; telemedicine named as an accelerator). Digital Transformation Concept 2024-2028. Cabinet Decree No. 640 of 24 Oct 2024 sets up a unified medical information system and a digital-health ecosystem, with the Centre for E-Health (CEZ) as technical operator. No dedicated "digital health 2023-2027" strategy was found, so that name in our task is probably wrong ([Decree 640 text](https://prg.kz/m/amp/document/38606362/), [Concept 2024-2028](https://digital.gov.kg/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/konczepcziya-czifrovoj-transformaczii-kyrgyzskoj-respubliki-na-2024-2028-gody.pdf), snippets). Partly verified.
- **UN Joint Programme "Bridging the Digital Health Divide" (WHO, UNDP, UNICEF, UNFPA; EU-funded; 2024-2027; $4.03M).** About 2.94M citizens use the Digital Health platform. ## 6. Languages

- Kyrgyz is the state language and Russian an official one. Both are widely used ([Constitution](https://www.gov.kg/ru/p/constitution), snippet).
- Medical education is multilingual: KSMA teaches in Kyrgyz, Russian and English; KRSU in Russian and English ([KSMA](https://www.kgma.kg/pdf/ma-eng-final-compressed.pdf); [KRSU](https://www.krsu.kg/en/universitet/novosti/krsu-povyshaev-uroven-obrazovaniya/) - snippets). Clinical vocabulary stays largely Russian or Latin/Greek. KRSU published a study on teaching Kyrgyz medical terms to Russian-medium students ([KRSU Bulletin](https://vestnik.krsu.kg/ky/archive/133/5711)). Partly verified.
- **No survey** of the language mix among feldshers and GPs was found (not found).
- **Terminology resources:** an official Russian-Kyrgyz explanatory dictionary of common medical terms (State Language Commission, 11 Oct 2024: [mamtil.gov.kg](https://mamtil.gov.kg/ky/post/999)); the KRSU "Big educational Russian-Kyrgyz dictionary of medical terms" (Amanalieva, 2022, 666 pp: [e-lib.krsu.kg](https://e-lib.krsu.kg/uploads/files/public/12229.pdf/en/info)); a 2012 Russian-Latin-Kyrgyz dictionary of about 1,700 terms ([okuma.kg](https://www.okuma.kg/books.php?id=574)); the MoH site hosts some clinical protocols in Kyrgyz ([egov.kg](https://egov.kg/kg/ministry/health)). All from search snippets, so Partly verified. We did not check the licences, so do not copy their text without permission.

## Implications for ECG-AI KG

1. **Fix the spec numbers before publishing.** Use CVD ~52% of deaths (2023, NSC) and ~16.5-17k deaths/yr. Use 1,059 FAPs. Drop or re-source "234.5/100k", "34-37% hypertension", "3rd/5th rank" and the "untapped lever" quote. Cite the 7.5%/30 min figure as a PCI-delay result for STEMI.
2. **Re-frame the competition.** The MoH/NCCIM is already moving on tele-ECG for FAPs and has agreed to buy AI-ECG "Digital FAP" kits. Position the app as an **offline photo-based assistant for existing paper ECG machines**, complementary to those programmes and able to hand data to a tele-ECG hub. Check whether it can be offered to NCCIM as a pilot.
3. **Design for the real paper.** Collect sample strips from 3-channel thermal machines (about 48 mm effective width, 25/50 mm/s) and test the digitiser on them. Don't assume 12-lead A4 layouts. Detect the paper speed and gain, or ask the user for them. ACS (ST changes) detection from 1-3 leads is very limited, so the product must say so clearly (researcher inference).
4. **Android first.** Release as a lean Android build for mid-range devices (Android 11+ covers about 90% of Android users by the Feb 2026 split). Keep iOS second, since the share could be 15-30%. Keep the model small and quantised.
5. **Offline-first, sync-later.** Even with 99% 4G coverage, mountain valleys and FAP buildings may have no signal. Queue cases and sync. Plan the data flow so it can plug into Tunduk and "Sanarip Clinic"/CEZ later, per Decree 640.
6. **Languages.** Ship ru/ky/en. Use the official 2024 State Language Commission dictionary as the terminology baseline. Have a Kyrgyz-speaking physician review the clinical text. Keep ECG terms in Latin/Russian alongside Kyrgyz (inference).
7. **Urgency and referral content.** Referral steps should reflect the KATLAB cath-lab network (NCCIM Bishkek; Balykchy, Talas and Naryn planned) and the "Acute MI 24/7" programme. Verify with MoH protocols and the cardiologist what the real transfer pathway is in each oblast.
