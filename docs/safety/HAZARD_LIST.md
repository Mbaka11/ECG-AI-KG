# Hazard list (ISO 14971-lite, draft v0.1)

Status: **draft**, Phase 2 · 2026-11-04. Companion to [INTENDED_USE](INTENDED_USE.md).
Method: simple hazard log as recommended in [research 07](../research/07-regulatory-ethics.md)
(s4, implication 1). Not a full ISO 14971 risk file. Severity and residual-risk ratings are the
developer's first judgement and are **pending cardiologist review**; no probability scale is
used yet. Clinical facts cite repo research notes.

Severity scale: **S1** minor (inconvenience); **S2** moderate (unneeded referral, delay without
lasting harm); **S3** serious (delayed care of a treatable condition); **S4** critical (death or
permanent harm possible).

Residual risk is what remains after the listed mitigations are implemented and tested; it is
"to be assessed" until the POC/validation data exist.

Owner/phase: P2 = scope and design, P3 = ML prototype (POC), P4 = app MVP, P5 = validation and pilot (as in
`docs/PLAN.md`; handover is P6). DEV = developer, CARD = cardiologist.

## Notes

- Hazards not yet in the table, to add when the design is fixed: wrong patient mix-up in
  forwarded cases; clock/time errors in the 10-minute and 120-minute targets (research 10);
  accessibility (small phone screens, glare in daylight); battery or storage exhaustion.
- Every mitigation above becomes a requirement with a test (IEC 62304-lite traceability) when
  the spec v2 requirements are written.
- The hazard list is reviewed at each phase gate and after any incident or model change.
- Severity, residual risk and the red/yellow/green thresholds need the cardiologist review
  planned in `docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`.
